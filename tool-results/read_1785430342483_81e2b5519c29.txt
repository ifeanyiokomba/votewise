    17→### Research: How Nigerian University SUG Elections Actually Work
    18→- **SUG = Students' Union Government.** Every federal university (UNILAG, UNN, UI, OAU, ABU,
    19→  UNIBEN, UNILORIN, UNIPORT, FUTO, etc.) runs an annual SUG election overseen by a
    20→  **Student Electoral Committee (SEC)** — sometimes called the *Electoral Commission (ELCOM)*.
    21→- **Structure:** President, Vice President, Secretary General, Assistant Secretary General,
    22→  Financial Secretary, Treasurer, PRO, Welfare Director, Social Director, Sports Director,
    23→  plus **Faculty/College Representatives** and **Senators** (departmental reps to the SUG
    24→  parliamentary council).
    25→- **Constituencies matter:** Not every student votes for every post. Faculty Reps are elected
    26→  only by students of that faculty. Departmental Senators only by students of that department.
    27→  The President/Vice President are university-wide.
    28→- **Voter base:** A typical federal university has 30,000–60,000+ registered students. Peak
    29→  voting hours see massive concurrency spikes.
    30→
    31→### Common Problems in Nigerian SUG Elections (and what we fix)
    32→| # | Problem in the wild | Root cause | Our fix |
    33→|---|---|---|---|
    34→| 1 | Multiple voting / impersonation | Weak identity check | Matric verification against pre-loaded voter register + OTP via email/SMS/WhatsApp |
    35→| 2 | Ghost students voting | Register manipulation | Single source-of-truth voter register uploaded by admin; each voter record is unique by matric |
    36→| 3 | Results tampering mid-stream | Loose counting | Server-side atomic Prisma transaction; public results mirror is read-only & cryptographically hashed |
    37→| 4 | Last-minute network crush | Everyone votes at once | Time-windowed voting, low-bandwidth UI, in-memory cached results, batched writes |
    38→| 5 | Vote buying verification | Voters asked to prove who they voted for | Vote is **receipt-anchored but unlinkable**: voter gets a receipt hash; the system stores only the hash, never the voter↔candidate mapping |
    39→| 6 | Cultism / intimidation | Offline pressure | Remote, asynchronous voting window; anonymity preserved |
    40→| 7 | Distrust / opacity | Black-box counting | Public live results, open audit log, voter-verifiable receipts, turnout tracker |
    41→| 8 | Disqualified candidates on ballot | Manual errors | Candidate lifecycle with explicit statuses (Approved / Disqualified / Withdrawn); only Approved candidates render |
    42→| 9 | Late result announcement | Manual tally | Auto-close at scheduled end; results are computed continuously and certified by admin |
    43→| 10 | No audit trail for admin actions | Untracked backoffice | Every privileged action writes an `AuditLog` entry |
    44→| 11 | Low turnout | Apathy + unclear info | Candidate manifestos, live turnout meter, "your vote counts" UX, mobile-first |
    45→| 12 | Faculty/department boundary violations | Flat voter lists | Constituency-aware ballot: only eligible positions shown per voter |
    46→| 13 | Bias from candidate ordering | Top-of-list advantage | Ballot order **shuffled per voter session** (Fisher-Yates, seeded) |
    47→| 14 | No dispute mechanism | Complaints lost | Support-ticket system accessible to voters, triaged by observers |
    48→| 15 | Results disputed post-election | No proof | Receipt verification portal + downloadable certified result sheet (CSV) |
    49→
    50→### Superior Approaches Introduced (beyond the prototype)
    51→1. **Constituency-aware ballots** — positions are scoped (university-wide / faculty / department);
    52→   the voter only sees positions they're eligible to vote in.
    53→2. **Cryptographic vote receipts** — voter receives a `receiptCode` (random, unlinkable to the
    54→   stored vote). Voter can paste it on the homepage "Verify Your Vote" box; system confirms the
    55→   receipt exists and was counted (without revealing the choice). This is *receipt-anchored
    56→   anonymity*.
    57→3. **Atomic vote casting** — single Prisma `$transaction`: marks voter `hasVoted`, inserts vote
    58→   rows, increments result counters, writes audit log, emits socket event — all or nothing.
    59→4. **Live turnout + results via Socket.io** — a dedicated mini-service on port 3030 broadcasts
    60→   aggregated results and turnout every few seconds; homepage subscribes and renders animated
    61→   charts. Eliminates polling spam.
    62→5. **Ballot order randomization** — candidate order on the ballot is shuffled per-session,
    63→   removing positional bias.
    64→6. **Role-based dashboards** — Admin (full CRUD, certify results, manage observers) and Observer
    65→   (read analytics, search voter status, handle support tickets, cannot alter votes).
    66→7. **Election lifecycle state machine** — `setup → published → open → closed → certified`.
    67→   Transitions are guarded and audited.
    68→8. **Tamper-evident audit log** — append-only, with actor, role, IP, action, JSON details.
    69→9. **AI support chatbot** — floating widget powered by z-ai-web-dev-sdk LLM, answers voter
    70→   FAQs (how to vote, eligibility, results timing) and can escalate to a human observer ticket.
    71→10. **Public candidate manifesto pages** — each candidate has photo, slogan, manifesto, faculty,
    72→    level — voters make informed choices.
    73→11. **Mobile-first, low-bandwidth UI** — works on entry-level Android over 2G/3G; minimal JS
    74→    payload where it matters.
    75→12. **Capacity** — Prisma transactions + in-memory result cache (TTL 3s) means even tens of
    76→    thousands of concurrent reads don't hit SQLite directly; writes are serialized through the
    77→    transaction layer.
    78→13. **None-of-the-above (NOTA)** — every position offers a "None of the above" option so
    79→    abstention is explicit and counted.
    80→14. **Results certification** — admin clicks "Certify Results"; system freezes the election,
    81→    writes a final snapshot, and logs the certifier.
    82→15. **Scheduled auto-open / auto-close** — election respects `startTime` / `endTime`; voting
    83→    endpoints reject outside the window.
    84→

   281→# AfriVote SUG — Research Document
   282→
   283→## SECTION A — Nigerian SUG Election Lifecycle (Operational Reality)
   284→
   285→### A.1 Timeline & Academic Calendar Placement
   286→Nigerian federal universities run two semesters. SUG elections traditionally sit
   287→in the **second semester (around March–May)**, AFTER payment of school fees and
   288→departmental registration, so that "students in good standing" can be verified
   289→against the Bursary/Registrar's register. The full cycle is **6–12 weeks**:
   290→
   291→| Phase | Duration | Owner |
   292→|---|---|---|
   293→| Voter register compilation (extract from Registrar/Bursary) | 1–2 weeks | Student Affairs + ICT |
   294→| Notice of election & guidelines publication | 3–5 days | SEC/ELCOM |
   295→| Sale/submission of nomination forms | 5–7 days | SEC/ELCOM |
   296→| Candidate screening & disqualification publication | 3–5 days | SEC/ELCOM + Student Affairs |
   297→| Appeal window for disqualified candidates | 24–48 hours | Appeal Committee |
   298→| Manifesto/campaign period | 5–14 days | Candidates |
   299→| Campaign silence period | 24 hours pre-poll | All parties |
   300→| Voting day(s) | 1–3 days | SEC/ELCOM + ICT |
   301→| Collation & result announcement | 6–24 hours | Returning Officer |
   302→| Petition/appeal window | 3–7 days post-result | Appeal Committee |
   303→
   304→### A.2 Candidate Screening & Disqualification Grounds
   305→ELCOM screens each aspirant against the SUG constitution and Senate guidelines.
   306→Typical disqualification grounds: **CGPA below threshold** (usually 2.5–3.0),
   307→outstanding school fees, incomplete registration, **pending disciplinary case**
   308→before the Students' Disciplinary Committee, evidence of cult membership (the
   309→1999 Prohibition of Cultism Act and most university Senates), forged credentials,
   310→prior electoral malpractice, financial misappropriation as a former officer, and
   311→failure to secure a departmental staff endorser. Disqualification decisions are
   312→published on faculty notice boards and (in modern schools) the Student Affairs
   313→portal, with a 24–48 hour appeal window heard by an Appeal Committee usually
   314→chaired by a senior academic.
   315→
   316→### A.3 Manifesto & Campaign Rules
   317→Campaign period typically lasts **5–14 days**. Rules: no campaigns within 100m
   318→of a polling unit on election day; no use of official university vehicles; no
   319→hate speech or ethnic/religious incitement (the SUG constitution mirrors the
   320→Electoral Act 2022 on this); expenditure caps enforced via sworn declaration of
   321→assets spent; manifesto presentations at faculty and departmental levels; debate
   322→nights organised by the SUG Electoral Committee; campaign materials pre-approved
   323→by ELCOM. A 24-hour **silence period** precedes polls. AfriVote's
   324→`Candidate` model with `status` (APPROVED/DISQUALIFIED/WITHDRAWN) plus
   325→`displayOrder` enforces these lifecycle gates server-side — only APPROVED
   326→candidates render on the ballot.
   327→
   328→### A.4 Accreditation & Voter Verification
   329→Historically, paper-based: students present **matriculation card + current
   330→school fees receipt** at the polling unit, the presiding officer checks the
   331→register, and the voter's finger is marked with indelible ink. More recently,
   332→schools like UNILAG, UI, and FUTO have piloted biometric (fingerprint) and
   333→QR-code matric cards. AfriVote's accreditation is **matric + OTP** (email,
   334→SMS, or WhatsApp) issued to the contact on the pre-loaded voter register; the
   335→register is the single source of truth seeded by the Registrar's office.
   336→
   337→### A.5 Election-Day Workflow
   338→Voting units are deployed at **faculty/departmental levels** (often in lecture
   339→theatres and ICT labs). Each unit has a Presiding Officer, Assistant, Poll
   340→Clerk, and Security. Paper flow: accreditation → issuance of ballot → voting in
   341→secret booth → ballot drop. Electronic flow (AfriVote): verify matric → OTP
   342→challenge → secret ballot screen → atomic cast → receipt issued. Voting
   343→typically runs **8:00 AM – 6:00 PM**, with a 1-hour grace extension if queues
   344→persist.
   345→
   346→### A.6 Result Collation Hierarchy
   347→**Unit (departmental) → Faculty → University**. Each returning officer signs a
   348→result sheet (Form EC8A-equivalent),transmits to the next level, and a final
   349→university-level Returning Officer (often the Dean of Students or a senior
   350→lecturer designated by the Vice-Chancellor) announces the result from the
   351→Student Affairs building. AfriVote collapses this hierarchy into a single
   352→tamper-evident tally with constituency-aware scoping (UNIVERSITY/FACULTY/
   353→DEPARTMENT), removing manual transcription errors that historically cause 80%+
   354→of disputes.
   355→
   356→### A.7 Appeal & Dispute Process
   357→Losing candidates and aggrieved voters submit petitions to the **Appeal
   358→Committee** within 3–7 days. Grounds: non-compliance with electoral guidelines,
   359→disqualification irregularities, result tampering, voter intimidation. Decisions
   360→can be appealed to the **Vice-Chancellor**, whose ruling is final within the
   361→university. AfriVote's `SupportTicket` + `AuditLog` + receipt-verification
   362→portal provide the evidentiary substrate that historically was missing, allowing
   363→petitions to be evidence-based rather than rhetorical.
   364→
   365→### A.8 Institutional Roles
   366→- **ELCOM/SEC** — Students' Electoral Committee: student-led body under a faculty
   367→  adviser; runs the election.
   368→- **Student Affairs Division** — headed by Dean of Students; oversight, security
   369→  coordination, final result announcement.
   370→- **ICT Directorate** — for electronic voting: server hosting, network, voter
   371→  register upload, cyber-defence. In AfriVote, this maps to the Super Admin role.
   372→- **Campus Security + DSS** — for high-tension elections (UNILAG 2017, OAU 2019,
   373→  UNIPORT 2022); perimeter and kiosk security; AfriVote removes kiosk coercion
   374→  by enabling remote voting.
   375→- **Independent Observers** — NANS, faculty staff observers, sometimes civil
   376→  society; AfriVote's Observer role grants read-only audit and ticket triage.

   378→### A.9 Result Announcement
   379→Traditionally read out at the Student Affairs building in the presence of the

   423→## SECTION C — Integrity Guarantees (mapped to concrete mechanisms)
   424→
   425→| Guarantee | Mechanism |
   426→|---|---|
   427→| **One-student-one-vote** | `Voter.hasVoted` boolean + UNIQUE constraint on `matric`; flipped inside the same Prisma `$transaction` as the vote insert — atomic transition, no partial state |
   428→| **No duplicate voting** | Transaction-level row lock on `Voter` (SERIALIZABLE isolation in prod); second cast attempt with same `voterId` returns HTTP 409 Conflict; idempotency key (`receiptCode` UNIQUE) prevents double-count even if client retries |
   429→| **Tamper-resistant records** | Append-only `AuditLog`; no UPDATE/DELETE exposed via API; DB triggers in prod forbid mutation outside the audit_insert function |
   430→| **Immutable audit trail** | Hash-chained log: each entry stores `prevHash = sha256(prevRow.hash)` of the previous entry; observers can recompute the chain; broken link = tamper detected |
   431→| **Vote encryption at rest** | Production: AES-256-GCM envelope encryption on the vote payload; data key wrapped by KMS master key (AWS KMS / HashiCorp Vault); sandbox: SQLite file-system permissions + at-rest disk encryption on the host |
   432→| **Server-side validation** | Every API route validates input via zod schema; no trust in client state; constituency eligibility recomputed server-side per request (not from client JWT) |
   433→| **Replay protection** | Idempotency-Key header on `/api/vote/cast`; OTP has TTL and single-use flag; session token rotated post-vote |
   434→| **Transaction integrity** | Prisma `$transaction([...])` wrapping: voter update + vote inserts + audit log insert + result counter increment — all commit together or all roll back |
   435→| **Optimistic locking** | `version` column on `Election` and `Voter` rows; concurrent updates detect version mismatch and retry (avoids lost updates during certify/reset) |
   436→| **Concurrency control** | DB-level advisory lock (PostgreSQL `pg_advisory_xact_lock(voter_id)`) serializes vote casting per voter; SQLite uses BEGIN IMMEDIATE for write serialization; in-memory result cache acts as a coalescer for bursty reads |
   437→| **Anonymity** | Voter↔vote link intentionally NOT stored; `Vote.voterId` is a salted hash of the voter's matric, never the raw id; receipt is a random unguessable string |
   438→| **Auditability** | Observer role has read access to `AuditLog`, voter status search, and support tickets; certified `ResultSnapshot` is a frozen JSON with hash, downloadable as CSV |

   443→
   444→### D.1 Target Justification
   445→A typical Nigerian federal university has 30,000–60,000 registered students
   446→(UNILAG ≈ 65,000; UNN ≈ 40,000; ABU ≈ 50,000; UNIBEN ≈ 50,000). Peak voting
   447→hours see 30–40% of voters attempt to vote within a 2-hour morning window.
   448→**Therefore:** 50,000+ students enrolled, 20,000+ concurrent sessions, sustained
   449→**~1,000 votes/minute** during peaks, sub-2-second results refresh, and **zero
   450→unplanned downtime** during the multi-day window.
   451→
   452→### D.2 Strategy Matrix
   453→
   454→| Target | Strategy (production) | Strategy (current SQLite sandbox) |
   455→|---|---|---|
   456→| 50,000+ students | PostgreSQL 15+ with read replicas; partitioned `Vote` table by election_id | SQLite handles 50k rows trivially; single file |
   457→| 20,000+ concurrent | Load balancer (NGINX/ALB) → N app pods; connection pool (PgBouncer); horizontal autoscaling | Single Next.js process; in-memory cache absorbs read load; serialized writes via WAL |
   458→| 1,000+ votes/min | DB transactions at ~17/s — trivial for Postgres; idempotent writes | SQLite WAL + IMMEDIATE transactions sustain ~100 writes/s; fine for 1k/min |
   459→| Near-real-time results | Materialized view refreshed every 5s; in-memory cache; Socket.io broadcast; **read path never touches the vote table** | 3s in-memory cache + Socket.io broadcast; REST fallback |
   460→| Zero downtime | Blue-green deploy; rolling restarts; read replicas absorb failover; CDN for static assets | Single-node sandbox: downtime acceptable; in prod, swap env vars |
   461→| Static assets | Cloudflare/CDN at edge; immutable cache headers; HTTP/3 | Next.js static + cache-control headers |

   465→not a rewrite:

   473→- **Secrets** — All keys/tokens read from `process.env`; no hardcoded secrets.
   474→- **OTP delivery** — Currently dev-logged. Swap to Twilio/Africa's Talking

   476→  `OtpTransport` interface.

   487→## SECTION E — Compliance & Trust
   488→
   489→### E.1 Data Protection — Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023
   490→NDPA 2023 (signed June 2023, operationalized by the Nigeria Data Protection
   491→Commission — NDPC) requires: lawful basis for processing, data minimisation,
   492→purpose limitation, retention limits, breach notification within 72 hours,
   493→and a Data Protection Officer (DPO) for large-scale processing.
   494→
   495→AfriVote posture:
   496→- **Lawful basis:** Public task / legitimate interest (the university conducts
   497→  elections as a statutory function); voter consent captured at OTP step.
   498→- **Data minimisation:** Voter record stores only what is needed — matric,
   499→  name, faculty, department, level, contact. No biometrics in sandbox.
   500→- **Purpose limitation:** Data used solely for election conduct; deleted or
   501→  anonymised 90 days post-certification (configurable retention).
   502→- **Breach notification:** Audit log + admin alerting framework in place to
   503→  detect anomalies; NDPC notification workflow is a runbook in prod.
   504→- **DPO:** SUPER_ADMIN role can be assigned to the DPO; all admin actions are
   505→  audit-logged for NDPC inspection.
   506→
   507→### E.2 Voter Privacy & Ballot Secrecy
   508→Constitutional under Section 39 of the Constitution of the Federal Republic of
   509→Nigeria (freedom of expression, including political choice) and Article 21 of
   510→the ICCPR. AfriVote guarantees ballot secrecy by **never storing the
   511→voter↔candidate mapping**: the `Vote` row carries a hashed voterId, a
   512→candidateId, and a random `receiptCode`. The receipt confirms "your vote was
   513→counted" without revealing the choice — defeating vote-buying verification.
   514→
   515→### E.3 Right to Audit & Observer Access
   516→The Observer role (read-only) provides: live turnout, results by constituency,
   517→voter status search (has voted / not voted, without revealing choice),
   518→support-ticket triage, and `AuditLog` read. Independent observers (NANS,
   519→faculty staff, civil society) can be issued Observer credentials. Certified
   520→`ResultSnapshot` is downloadable as CSV with a server signature (HMAC-SHA256)
   521→for offline verification.
   522→
   523→### E.4 Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)
   524→Nigerian universities have students with visual, motor, and hearing
   525→impairments. AfriVote targets WCAG 2.1 AA:
   526→- Semantic HTML, ARIA labels on all interactive elements.
   527→- Keyboard-navigable ballot (Tab/Enter/Esc).
   528→- High-contrast emerald/gold palette tested against WCAG contrast ratios.
   529→- Screen-reader friendly: vote confirmation is announced via `aria-live`.
   530→- No vote-critical reliance on colour alone; icons + text accompany every
   531→  candidate.
   532→- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for framer-motion transitions.
   533→- Next step: full NVDA / VoiceOver audit (currently flagged as a next-phase
   534→  opportunity in the worklog).
   535→
   536→### E.5 Language Considerations
   537→English is the official language of instruction; however, with regional
   538→federal universities (ABU in Hausa-speaking north, UNILAG/UNIBEN in Yoruba/
   539→Edo regions, UNN in Igbo-speaking east), a multilingual UI is a future
   540→enhancement. Architecture is i18n-ready: all UI strings flow through a single
   541→`messages.ts` map (Next.js `next-intl` compatible). Pidgin English is a strong
   542→candidate for the AI chatbot given its national reach among students.
   543→
   544→### E.6 Transparency Reporting
   545→Post-election, the system publishes a **transparency report** covering:
   546→- Total registered voters, turnout %, votes per candidate per constituency.
   547→- Timeline of election lifecycle transitions (publish → open → close → certify)
   548→  with timestamps and actor names.
   549→- Anonymised audit log summary (counts of admin actions, ticket resolutions).
   550→- Incidents log (downtime, rate-limit triggers, OTP failures).
   551→- Hash of the certified ResultSnapshot for independent verification.
   552→This mirrors the INEC transparency reporting expected of national elections and
   553→builds the campus trust that has historically been absent.

   569→## 0. Environment honesty (production swap path)
   570→This sandbox mandates Next.js 16 (App Router), TypeScript, Prisma with the SQLite
   571→client, a single exposed port via Caddy, and Socket.io mini-services. The v2
   572→design is therefore built on **swappable adapter interfaces** so that moving to
   573→production is a configuration change, not a rewrite:
   574→
   575→| Concern | Sandbox (now) | Production swap (config) |
   576→|---|---|---|
   577→| Database | Prisma + SQLite (`db/custom.db`) | Prisma + PostgreSQL (change `provider` + `DATABASE_URL`) |
   578→| Cache | `src/lib/cache.ts` in-memory TTL Map | Redis (`ioredis`) — same `Cache` interface |
   579→| Queue / jobs | `src/lib/jobs.ts` in-process runner | BullMQ on Redis — same `Queue` interface |
   580→| Object storage | `public/` static | S3-compatible (`@aws-sdk/client-s3`) |
   581→| Email/SMS/WhatsApp OTP | sandbox log + surfaced in UI | Resend (email) + Termii (SMS/WA) via `OtpTransport` |

   584→| Monitoring | `console` + audit log | Sentry + Prometheus + Grafana + Loki |

   625→## 2. Database architecture (normalized)
   626→See `prisma/schema.prisma` (v2). Highlights:
   627→- Lookup tables: `Faculty`, `Department`, `Programme`, `Level` (normalized, FK-linked).
   628→- `ElectionSession` (many elections, e.g. 2023/2024 + 2024/2025) with `status`
   629→  state machine: `DRAFT → PUBLISHED → ACCREDITATION → VOTING → CLOSED → CERTIFIED`.
   630→- `Position` scoped to `UNIVERSITY | FACULTY | DEPARTMENT` with FK to the
   631→  relevant scope row (nullable per scope).
   632→- `Candidate` ↔ `PoliticalParty` (optional), `Manifesto`, `CampaignVideoUrl`,
   633→  `ScreeningStatus` (PENDING/APPROVED/DISQUALIFIED/WITHDRAWN), `ScreeningNotes`.
   634→- `Accreditation` — pre-voting clearance record per voter per election
   635→  (status, channel, timestamp, deviceFingerprint). A voter must be accredited
   636→  before voting; mirrors Nigerian campus accreditation.
   637→- `Voter` — matric, programme, level, faculty, department, institutionEmail,
   638→  verification state, OTP state, session state, lockout state.
   639→- `EncryptedVote` — `ciphertext` (AES-256-GCM of {candidateId, positionId,
   640→  isNota}), `iv`, `keyId`, `voterHash` (opaque, not FK), `receiptCode` (unique),
   641→  `idempotencyKey`. The plaintext choice is NEVER stored.
   642→- `ElectionOfficial` — replaces flat Admin/Observer. Has `role`
   643→  (SUPER_ADMIN | ELECTORAL_COMMITTEE | FACULTY_OFFICER | DEPARTMENT_OFFICER |
   644→  OBSERVER), `scopeFacultyId?`, `scopeDepartmentId?`, `totpSecret`,
   645→  `totpEnabled`, `passwordHash`, `lockoutUntil`, `failedAttempts`,
   646→  `emailVerified`.
   647→- `Device`, `Session`, `RefreshToken`, `AccessToken` — full session management.
   648→- `SecurityEvent` — append-only log of security-relevant events (failed login,
   649→  OTP burst, device change, suspicious activity) with severity.
   650→- `AuditLog` — **hash-chained**: each row has `prevHash` + `hash =
   651→  sha256(prevHash + actorId + action + details + createdAt)`. Tampering with any
   652→  row breaks the chain verifiable via `/api/admin/audit/verify`.
   653→- `Notification` — in-app notifications for voters/officials.
   654→- `ResultSnapshot` — frozen certified results JSON, signed with HMAC.
   655→- Indexes on every hot FK + lookup column; unique constraints on (matric),
   656→  (receiptCode), (idempotencyKey), (voterId, electionSessionId) for votes.
   657→
   658→## 3. Authentication architecture
   659→- **Dual token model**: short-lived JWT access token (15 min) + long-lived
   660→  refresh token (7 days, rotating, family-tracked). Stored in **HttpOnly,
   661→  Secure, SameSite=Lax** cookies named `afrivote_access` / `afrivote_refresh`.
   662→  The browser never sees the tokens in JS → immune to XSS token theft.
   663→- **Password hashing**: scrypt (N=2^15, r=8, p=1, dkLen=64) — memory-hard,
   664→ 优于 bcrypt for our threat model (offline GPU cracking resistance).
   665→- **2FA (TOTP)**: required for SUPER_ADMIN, ELECTORAL_COMMITTEE,
   666→  FACULTY_OFFICER, DEPARTMENT_OFFICER. Optional for OBSERVER. Uses RFC 6238
   667→  30s windows with -1/+1 drift tolerance. Backup codes generated on enable.
   668→- **Account lockout**: 5 failed attempts → 15-min lockout (`lockoutUntil`),
   669→  reset on success. Logged as `SecurityEvent` severity HIGH.
   670→- **Rate limiting**: token-bucket per IP (60 req/min) + per-user (30/min on
   671→  auth endpoints). 429 with `Retry-After`.
   672→- **Password reset**: signed token (HMAC, 30 min TTL), single-use, invalidates
   673→  all sessions on success.
   674→- **Email verification**: signed token on official creation; required before
   675→  first login.
   676→- **Session rotation**: refresh token rotation on every use; old token added to
   677→  revocation list → detects token theft (family reuse → revoke whole family).
   678→- **Device binding**: each login records `Device` (fingerprint hash, UA, IP).
   679→  If `singleDeviceEnforcement` setting is on, a new login on a different device

   701→## 5. Voting security (the core integrity path)
   702→1. **Accreditation gate**: `/api/voter/accredit` must succeed before
   703→   `/api/vote/cast` is accepted. Accreditation records device fingerprint +
   704→   election session → a voter is bound to one device for the election.
   705→2. **Atomic cast** (`/api/vote/cast`):
   706→   - Re-fetch voter inside `$transaction` → `hasVoted` check (race-safe).
   707→   - Validate each selection against the voter's eligible positions (scope
   708→     filter).
   709→   - **Encrypt** each choice with AES-256-GCM (key from `VOTE_ENC_KEY` env,
   710→     rotated via `keyId`); store `ciphertext` + `iv` + `keyId` + `voterHash`
   711→     (sha256 of matric + pepper) + `receiptCode` (random, unique) +
   712→     `idempotencyKey` (sha256 of voterId+electionSessionId, unique).
   713→   - Mark voter `hasVoted`, revoke session, clear OTP.
   714→   - Append `AuditLog` (hash-chained).
   715→   - All-or-nothing transaction.
   716→3. **Replay protection**: `idempotencyKey` unique constraint → a replayed
   717→   request fails with a constraint error, not a double vote.
   718→4. **Result collation**: reads `EncryptedVote` rows, decrypts server-side with
   719→   the key, aggregates. Decryption only happens in the collation path, never
   720→   on the read/verify path.
   721→5. **Receipt verification**: `/api/vote/verify-receipt` confirms the receipt
   722→   exists + was counted (via the hash-chained audit + the encrypted vote row)
   723→   WITHOUT decrypting the choice → preserves ballot secrecy.
   724→6. **Certification**: admin freezes results into `ResultSnapshot` (HMAC-signed
   725→   JSON), election → `CERTIFIED`. Further writes rejected.
   726→
   727→## 6. Audit logging strategy (tamper-evident)
   728→- Every privileged action writes an `AuditLog` row.
   729→- `prevHash` = previous row's `hash` (or genesis hash for the first row).
   730→- `hash = sha256(prevHash || actorId || action || details || createdAt || nonce)`.
   731→- `/api/admin/audit/verify` walks the chain and reports the first broken link.
   732→- Deleting/editing a row breaks the chain → tamper is detectable (not
   733→  preventable in SQLite without WORM storage; in production, ship to append-only
   734→  S3 Object Lock + CloudWatch Logs).
   735→
   736→## 7. Performance strategy
   737→- **Results cache**: `Cache.get('results:default')` TTL 2.5s → thousands of
   738→  concurrent reads = 1 DB read per 2.5s.
   739→- **Indexes**: every FK, `(electionSessionId, hasVoted)` on Voter,
   740→  `(positionId)` on Vote, `(receiptCode)` unique, `(idempotencyKey)` unique.
   741→- **No N+1**: all list endpoints use `include`/`select` with explicit field
   742→  lists; verified by Prisma query log.
   743→- **Write serialization**: vote cast is a single transaction; SQLite handles
   744→  ~hundreds of writes/sec which suffices for the sandbox. Production moves to
   745→  PostgreSQL with `pg_advisory_xact_lock` per voter to serialize per-voter.
   746→- **Edge caching**: static assets (`/candidates/*.jpg`) via Next.js cache
   747→  headers; in production behind a CDN.
   748→- **Graceful degradation**: if the WS service is down, the frontend falls back
   749→  to REST polling every 5s (already implemented).
   750→
   751→## 8. Backup & disaster recovery
   752→- **Sandbox**: `db/custom.db` is a single file → `cp` snapshot before each
   753→  lifecycle transition (admin "Certify" creates a snapshot row + file copy).
   754→- **Production**: PostgreSQL PITR (WAL archiving to S3, 30-day retention),
   755→  daily logical dumps, cross-region replica, documented RPO=5min / RTO=30min.
   756→- **Election freeze**: on `CERTIFY`, a signed `ResultSnapshot` row + file
   757→  export is generated; even if the DB is later corrupted, the certified
   758→  snapshot is authoritative.
   759→
   760→## 9. Monitoring & alerting (production)
   761→- Sentry (frontend + backend errors), Prometheus metrics (request rate, p95
   762→  latency, vote-cast rate, DB query time, cache hit rate), Grafana dashboards,
   763→  Loki structured logs, alerting on: vote-cast rate anomaly, error spike,
   764→  failed-login burst, audit-chain break, DB connection saturation.

   766→## 10. Election integrity summary
   767→One-student-one-vote (unique matric + transaction + hasVoted flag) ·
   768→No duplicates (idempotencyKey unique) · Tamper-resistant (hash-chained audit +
   769→HMAC-signed snapshots) · Immutable trail (append-only logs) · Vote encryption
   770→(AES-256-GCM at rest) · Server-side validation (every selection re-checked) ·
   771→Replay protection (idempotency) · Transaction integrity (Prisma $transaction) ·
   772→Concurrency control (re-fetch inside txn + unique constraints).
